## What this folder is for

This is not just product documentation. It is meant to answer typical exit and diligence questions:

- What exactly is the company/product buying party acquiring?
- What works today in the product and what does not?
- What technical and operational dependencies exist?
- What commercial thesis supports future value?
- Which risks remain and how transferable is the business?

## What is intentionally not claimed

This folder does **not** claim:

- audited financial performance
- legally complete company-level data room readiness
- proof of market traction beyond the documents and product assets in this repository

Where evidence is missing, the documents state that explicitly.
