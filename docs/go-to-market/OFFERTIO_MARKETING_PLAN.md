# Offertio Marketing Plan

## Goal
Get the first paying DACH pilot customers for Offertio and validate that the product can convert from real use into real revenue.

## Positioning
Offertio is the fastest and most professional way for small DACH businesses to create offers and invoices directly after a customer appointment.

### Short version
Offerten und Rechnungen direkt nach dem Termin — einfacher und günstiger als alte CRM-Dinosaurier.

### Longer version
Offertio hilft kleinen Betrieben in CH, DE und AT, direkt nach dem Kundentermin professionelle Offerten und Rechnungen zu erstellen — mobil, einfach und ohne CRM-Ballast.

## Best first target customers
Start narrow.

### Priority segments
1. Cleaning companies
2. Small handwork/service businesses
3. One-person or small teams doing repeated on-site jobs

### Good signals
- owner-led business
- sends many small/medium quotes
- currently uses Word, PDF templates, WhatsApp, Excel, or old software
- responds slowly after appointments because admin happens later

## Pilot offer
### Founder pilot
- guided setup
- direct founder support
- discounted early plan
- close feedback loop
- priority for requested improvements

### Suggested pricing
- 14-day free usage or assisted trial
- then low-friction early adopter price
- example:
  - CHF/EUR 19–29/month founder pilot
  - later standard price higher

The goal is not maximizing price immediately.
The goal is proving willingness to pay.

## Go-to-market order
### 1. Handpicked direct outreach
Do not start broad.
Start with 30–100 handpicked businesses.

### 2. Demo-first selling
The product is easier to sell through a short demo than a long explanation.

### 3. Pilot onboarding
Get a few real businesses using it, then refine the product and message from their objections.

## Outreach channels
### A. Cold email
Use low-volume, high-relevance outreach.
Do not spray.

### B. Phone + follow-up email
Very strong for local service businesses.
Short call -> send simple follow-up.

### C. WhatsApp / direct message where culturally appropriate
Only if contact context makes sense.
Keep it short and respectful.

### D. Local/network referrals
Accountants, web designers, small business consultants, trade contacts.

### E. Founder-led demos
Short 10-minute walkthroughs.
Show real flow, not slide decks.

## Cold outreach angles
### Angle 1 — speed after the appointment
You already did the work. Offertio helps you get the document out before the evening admin starts.

### Angle 2 — simpler than old CRM tools
No CRM ballast. No giant setup. Just faster offers and invoices.

### Angle 3 — more professional appearance
Better-looking, cleaner customer-facing documents without more admin work.

## Demo structure
Keep demos under 10 minutes.

### Demo flow
1. Problem in one sentence
2. Show login/onboarding quickly
3. Show creating a quote directly after an appointment
4. Show sending / PDF / professional output
5. Show upgrade value only briefly
6. Ask one closing question:
   Would this save you time in your real workflow?

## Objection handling
### “We already use something.”
Klar — die Frage ist eher, ob es für diesen einen Moment wirklich schnell genug ist.
Offertio ersetzt nicht alles. Es löst genau den Dokumenten-Moment einfacher.

### “We don’t want another complex tool.”
Genau deshalb ist Offertio bewusst kein CRM-Monster.
Der Fokus liegt nur auf Offerten und Rechnungen direkt nach dem Termin.

### “We do this later in the office.”
Das ist genau der Punkt, den wir verkürzen wollen.
Weniger zweiter Arbeitstag, schnellerer professioneller Eindruck beim Kunden.

### “We’re too small for software.”
Gerade kleine Betriebe profitieren am meisten, wenn ein Tool wenig Reibung macht und keinen langen Setup-Prozess braucht.
