# Offertio Growth Plan

## Objective
Build a repeatable growth system that helps Offertio acquire, activate, and convert small Swiss service businesses and trades without relying on random marketing effort.

The long-term goal is to use **OpenClaw as the Growth OS / agent layer** that supports research, copy generation, experimentation, analysis, and iteration.

---

## 1. Core Market Thesis

### Target audience
Primary audience:
- small Swiss businesses
- handworkers / trades
- cleaning companies
- local service businesses

Audience traits:
- not highly technical
- low tolerance for software complexity
- wants professional output with minimal admin burden
- works on-site, mobile, between customer visits

### Core problem
These businesses often lose time in the gap between:
- finishing the job
- preparing the quote or invoice
- following up professionally

The pain is not “missing software features”.
The pain is:
- too much office work
- too much evening catch-up
- fragmented information
- delayed follow-up
- unprofessional document flow

### Core promise
Offertio helps small businesses:
- reduce office/admin burden
- respond faster after customer visits
- create more professional customer-facing documents
- work mobile-first

---

## 2. Positioning

### Positioning direction
Offertio is not “AI accounting software”.
Offertio should be positioned as:

> a calm, mobile-first, professional document workflow for small service businesses that want less office work and more operational clarity.

### Messaging pillars
1. Less office work
2. Faster follow-up after visits
3. Professional customer-facing output
4. Mobile-first everyday use
5. Swiss / DACH trust signals

### Messaging anti-patterns
Avoid:
- generic AI SaaS language
- startup-bro tone
- too much feature dumping
- unsupported speed claims
- fake social proof
- internal/product-note style copy

---

## 3. Offer Strategy

### Entry offer
- Free plan / low-friction start
- immediate product use
- clear free-to-Pro upgrade path

### Pro upgrade narrative
Pro should feel like:
- more volume
- more operational calm
- stronger professional output
- better scaling for a growing business

### Offer principles
- low risk
- easy to try
- no complex setup narrative
- clear business value

## 7. OpenClaw as Growth OS

### Role
OpenClaw should become the system that supports:
- research
- messaging
- ad ideation
- landing iteration
- experiment planning
- reporting

### Best initial use case
Use OpenClaw as a **human-in-the-loop growth assistant**, not as a fully autonomous marketer.

### OpenClaw should do automatically
- competitor research
- message research
- idea generation
- content drafting
- experiment suggestions
- weekly summaries
- landing variant proposals

### OpenClaw should require approval for
- publishing posts
- sending emails
- contacting leads
- launching ads
- spending money
- making public claims
